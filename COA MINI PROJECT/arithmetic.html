Execution Flow — Six Interactive Stages

STAGE 1: LOAD
1 LOAD

Load inputs A and B into internal registers.

2 DECODE

Decode opcode (000 for ADD, 001 for SUB).

3 ENABLE

Enable 8-bit Adder/Subtractor core.

4 COMPUTE

Compute binary sum/difference with flags.

5 FLAGS

Evaluate CCR flags (C, Z, N, V).

6 WRITE

Latch result into Result Register [ACC].
